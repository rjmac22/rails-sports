## Pitch-first operating rules

1. Before writing a full new article, spend at most 1–3 hours verifying its core scene, evidence and consequence.
2. Write a 100–200 word targeted pitch; check the outlet's existing coverage and submission rules.
3. Once commissioned, agree scope, deadline, byline, payment, exclusivity and rights; only then invest in deep research.
4. Record each actual sent email, follow-up, response, commission and publication here. Avoid claiming simultaneous submission is prohibited or permitted without checking specific outlet rules.
5. A published credit matters more than a high fee at this stage, but preserve editorial integrity and byline.
6. For football Draft 3: the latest article was not located in the rails-sports default-branch repository on 29 Oct 2026; the earlier docs/drafts/22_football_collaborative.md covers a different earlier scope. Do not mistake it for the current final manuscript.
